#### **Able Bodied is expanding!** 

[Sam Kolb](/board-of-directors/), one of our board members, has generously offered his home in Sebastopol as a second Able Bodied handcycling lesson site. He has a ramp in his garage to make transferring easy and is very close to a pretty epic bike path. You can handcycle by vineyards on a long winding bike path that is fairly flat and mellow. 

Come celebrate the launch of this second site at [Ragle park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3qjPVtFeHArZLpKB8) on Sunday October 20th at 10am (more details in the flyer below)

#### Dual Motor FULL SUSPENSION Lasher Handcycle

For the Sebastopol Able Bodied location we will be featuring the all new, dual motor Lasher handcycle. This 3,000W beast is capable of going up any mountain and back down with a butter smooth ride using the full suspension.

 This is the most capable handcycle we have ever had. Sign up for a free lesson here (link coming soon).
