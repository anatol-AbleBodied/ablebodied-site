Daniel Mila, a loving father to an ten year old son, a lifelong outdoorsman and athlete, was paralyzed in car accident in December 2022.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-20-at-9.59.35-PM.jpeg)

He inherited his love of the outdoors from his grandfather and great uncle, who participated in dozens of rides across Iowa in the annual [RAGBRAI](https://ragbrai.com/) ride. This mass biking event attracts tens of thousands of riders from across the world and is a sight to see as a river of riders overtake the roads. Daniel went on the ride several times, but thought he might never do so again after he was paralyzed. 

Little did he know that electric assist handcycling could help him complete the 406 mile long journey and allow him to bike along his able bodied family members. After being introduced to electric assist handcycling at our [handcycle by the ocean Santa Cruz](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/16W7WK1sKgU) [](/2024/06/01/handcycle-by-the-ocean-in-santa-cruz-on-june-15th/)event, Daniel became one of our first Get 1 Help 2 participants.

Daniel’s uncle completed the RAGBRAI ride 25 times, but unfortunately passed away last year. Daniel wants to join the rest of his family this year for an epic ride in his uncle’s memory. He has committed to raising $4,000 for his electric assist handcycle, which he plans to ride in RAGBRAI 2025. This handcycle will enable him to go on more epic rides and enjoy nature as he did when he was able bodied.
