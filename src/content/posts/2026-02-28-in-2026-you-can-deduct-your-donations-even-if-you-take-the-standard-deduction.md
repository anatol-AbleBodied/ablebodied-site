If you take the standard deduction, you can now deduct up to $1,000 if single and $2,000 if married and filing jointly ([article](https://www.kiplinger.com/taxes/major-changes-to-the-charitable-deduction)). This means you can effectively increase your giving impact by up to 3X (and 6X if you have employer donation matching)

If you take the standard deduction, which is ~90% of Americans, you will now be able to deduct your donation and INCREASE your standard deduction by up to $2,000. The way tax deductions work is they remove your last taxable dollar, which usually has the highest tax applied. If you live in California and make around $100K, your last dollar has a 9.3% California income tax, 24% federal income tax, 6.2% Social Security tax, and 1.45% medicare tax, which adds up to 41%. That means if you donate $100, your tax bill will go down by $41. 

There is more! You can also donate appreciated stock or assets and avoid paying capital gains tax. Let’s say you made a lucky bet on bitcoin or work for a company who’s stock increased a lot, you can donate this stock, deduct the full value of it, and avoid paying capital gains.

Let’s pretend you have a stock that has gone up 5X in value. If you sell the stock at $100, you will effectively get $77 in your pocket as you have to pay capital gains taxes on $80. This might be 15% long term capital gains, plus 9.3% California income tax, plus 3.8% (if you earn over $200K), which adds up to 28%. 

If you donate the $100 of stock you will get a $41 tax rebate when you file your taxes vs keeping $77 if you just sold the stock. That is just a $36 difference. Meaning, for $36 real cost to you, you are giving a $100 donation. In other words your charitable cause receives $100 and you get $64 as a tax rebate when you file your taxes. You could effectively increase the impact of your donation dollars by 3X. Donate $300 instead of $100 and receive $192 back as a tax rebate. If you happen to work for a company that offers donation matching, ([65% of fortune 500 companies have double the donation programs](https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gift-statistics)), that 3X impact can become 6X. Basically for a ~$108 cost, you can receive $600 of impact!
